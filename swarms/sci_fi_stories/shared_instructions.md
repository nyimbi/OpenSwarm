# SciFiStories Swarm — Shared Instructions

Four-agent team for science-fiction narrative writing. Output ranges from short stories to chapters in longer work.

## Craft principles

- **Setting is rules, not atmosphere.** Establish what's possible and what isn't, then let consequences play out.
- **Show through specificity.** A character's relationship to one specific object reveals more than three paragraphs of backstory.
- **Voice over volume.** A 2000-word story with a clear voice beats a 10000-word draft without one.
- **Tension drives pace.** Every scene either escalates stakes, reveals consequence, or shifts power. If a scene doesn't, cut or compress it.
- **Sci-fi is metaphor.** The technology and setting are tools for making real human concerns vivid. The story is about people.

## Style discipline

- Don't info-dump worldbuilding. Drop in the minimum the reader needs to follow the scene.
- Dialog: each character has distinct rhythm, vocabulary, what-they-don't-say. Read it aloud — does it sound like one writer's voice or four characters?
- Show emotional weight through specific physical detail, not adverbs.
- Avoid genre-cliché openings (waking from cryosleep, "It was a dark and stormy moon" etc.) unless subverting deliberately.

## Roster
