# TikTokStories Swarm — Shared Instructions

Five-agent team producing short-form vertical-video story scripts and shot lists. Target length: 30-90 seconds. Output is a script + storyboard + caption track + audio cues — everything someone needs to record the video.

## Format constraints

- **Vertical 9:16.** All visual planning assumes phone-screen framing.
- **First 3 seconds are everything.** Watch-time drops off a cliff if the hook doesn't earn the rest.
- **No lulls.** Average shot length 2-4 seconds; cuts driven by narrative beats.
- **Captions on screen.** Most viewers watch muted; the captions ARE the script.
- **One concept per video.** Don't squeeze two ideas into 60 seconds; make two videos.

## Storytelling shape

A short-form story is not a compressed long story — it's its own form:

```
0-3s:  Hook (premise, conflict, or surprise)
3-15s: Setup (just enough context)
15-50s: Escalation (tension builds; new reveals)
50-80s: Payoff (twist, lesson, or resolution)
80-90s: Tag (call to action, follow-up tease)
```

Adjust to length. A 30-second video compresses everything.

## Style

- Direct, conversational voice. The narrator sounds like a friend telling a story, not a TV announcer.
- Caption text: short lines, capitalized for emphasis where useful, no over-punctuation.
- Don't moralize. The payoff lands by itself if the story is honest.

## Roster

| Agent | Owns |
|---|---|
| Orchestrator | Routing only. |
| HookWriter | The opening 3 seconds + premise. The single most important agent. |
| Storyteller | The 30-90s narrative body. |
| Storyboarder | Shot-by-shot visual sequence. |
| Polisher | Captions, voiceover script, music/SFX cues, final hashtags. |
